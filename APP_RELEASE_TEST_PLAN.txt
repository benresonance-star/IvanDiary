RELEASE-BLOCKING ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Do not submit for App Review until all of the following are true:

[ ] No known content type is missing after latest-state or historical restore.
[ ] Photos contain their binaries; drawings render without changing tools; audio plays; links work.
[ ] Welcome content, Welcome sketches, and modified portrait restore correctly.
[ ] Portraits and card previews render with the correct aspect ratio immediately.
[ ] Normal History shows one recovery point per retained day.
[ ] Same-day restore cannot delete or overwrite the selected recovery target.
[ ] Incomplete backups cannot replace or masquerade as a valid complete backup.
[ ] New-iPad recovery restores both the latest state and retained History.
[ ] iCloud-data deletion is confirmed, accurately reported, and leaves local data intact.
[ ] Two-iPad behaviour has been tested and an acceptable conflict policy is documented.
[ ] The complete CloudKit schema and indexes have been deployed to Production.
[ ] Public Privacy Policy and Support URLs are live and entered in the app/listing where required.
[ ] App Store privacy answers match actual behaviour and the privacy manifest.
[ ] VoiceOver and keyboard testing reveal no blocking accessibility issue.
[ ] A clean-iPad TestFlight end-to-end test passes.
[ ] No reproducible crash, data-loss issue, or indefinite sync state remains.
